<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">…As soon as the plume of smoke cleared up, Ram felt a rare sense of helplessness.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram: […]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">She opened her pale crimson eyes, straining them as she peered into the room which still had smoke hovering over it. However, even as she did so, searching for something amongst the emptiness, the ones which she was seeking were nowhere to be seen.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">She looked for those people in the room where a large hole had appeared, someone moving, or someone in black, along with the adorable blue one; however, they were nowhere to be found…</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">???: […Ram!]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram’s shoulder was grabbed from behind as she’d tried to take a step forward, bringing her back to her senses. She realized that she’d been about to step through a cracked bit of the floor. If she hadn’t been stopped in her tracks, she might have fallen.</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">How clumsy of her, to literally not even see what was beneath her feet.</span></i></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">???: [I’m glad you’re safe Ram… And Patrasche-chan, are you okay as well?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram: [Emilia… sama…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram bit her lip. Meanwhile, the one who’d grabbed her by the shoulder was Emilia, who </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">should </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">have been on the upper floors. While panting slightly, Emilia checked to make sure Ram was okay, as well as how the black Earth Dragon, Patrasche, who was crouched down behind them, was getting on.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Finally, while looking at them, Ram regained enough of her composure to take in the state of her surroundings.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The corridor in what should have been a robust stone tower had collapsed, with the floor and ceiling both smashed to pieces. She could see the sky and sand of the sand-sea from across the wall, which now had a hole in it; a parched breeze was blowing in as a result.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">However, the place that had gotten damaged the most was the room where Ram, Patrasche and co. had been staying in until just before ― That’s to say, the room they’d so dubbed the “Green Room”.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram: […]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The room had been chock-full of grass and ivy, covering the walls, floor, and even going up all the way to the ceiling. Nevertheless, the room was now unrecognizable from its original state, with its natural erosion of many, many years torn up root and branch.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That included its beds, which were woven from grass and ivy, as well as the faint light the Spirit’s presence gave off; all of it.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That, and their biggest problem was…</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: [Ram… Where did Subaru and Rem go? They’re safe, right?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Standing by Ram’s side, Emilia peered into the area where the Green Room had previously stood. Ram wasn’t able to answer her question straight away, even though she knew that her painful silence itself served as an answer.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　　&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp; </span></p>
<p style="text-align: center;"><span style="font-weight: 400;">△▼△▼△▼△</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">???: […First of all, let’s try ‘n sort out what happened. We’re not gonna be able ta talk about things calmly unless we do tha’.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Looking around at everyone who’d gathered within the room as she said that, Anastasia took charge of the situation.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia’s white fox scarf ― Echidna ― was still wrapped around her. She brushed her fingers through her slightly dusty fur as she spoke.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [So, about that black shadow that attacked the tower jus’ before…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius: [It’s gone, at least for now. Thanks to the power of the Divine Dragon, Volcanica, I’d say.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Echidna: [Both the Dragon and the shadow’s movements were so sudden, it made us really wonder what the heck was happening, huh.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Inside the tower, the ones who’d come back safe and sound began to share what had happened, starting first with Anastasia, and then Julius and Echidna following her lead.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Much like Anastasia and Echidna, Julius’ white Knight garb was also covered in grime. However, that wasn’t just because of the heated battle that had taken place in the tower, it was also heavily due to the effects of the aforementioned event.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nevertheless, none of these three had ended up with any extra injuries; on that point itself, you could say it was a relief.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">That in itself was thanks to the achievement wrought by the Divine Dragon, Volcanica, on the top-most floor of the tower.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: [Volcanica cleaved the looming shadow with its breath, I suppose. If not for that, we would have probably been in for a world of hurt, in fact.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia gave Beatrice a nod in response to what she’d said, as the latter folded her short arms together.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In all honesty, when Volcanica had unleashed its breath so suddenly, Anastasia had uncharacteristically resigned herself for death. Things had happened so quickly, that there hadn’t been any way to deal with it.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Though of course she couldn’t say that she’d completely relaxed her guard just because of that single breath.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius: [The Divine Dragon’s response to the crisis dawning upon us was simply magnificent, but even so, the state it’s in isn’t great, that remains unchanged still… Currently though, it seems to be keeping itself quite obedient and following Emilia-sama’s instructions.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [Out of consideration for Julius, I’mma try ‘n avoid callin’ ‘em “senile”… But, ‘tis all the same as the Divine Dragon-san bein’ a scary‘un. Anyways, as for what happened, it’s exactly as Beatrice-chan says.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: [I suppose.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The only one who could have dealt with a situation as abrupt as that was Volcanica. </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">What would have become of them now, had the Dragon not blown away the looming shadow for them.</span></i></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Yet, they couldn’t start jumping around in joy…</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">???: [There was a to~n of ‘em. But, they all went towards Onii-san and stuff…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [Meili-chan.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meili: […Ah, my apo~logies.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: […]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meili didn’t take care with her word choice, almost as if she were upset, and for that, Anastasia chided her. Meili gave her a sincere apology as she looked towards Ram and Emilia, both having kept their silence.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In hindsight, the ones who’d received the damage from that shadow had been the two from the Emilia Camp ― Subaru and Rem ― Thus, the damage the Anastasia Camp had suffered was practically zilch.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [Though it’s not like I can actually say that, we’re all travellin’ together after all.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia muttered that to herself before then catching a glimpse of Julius and Echidna. They had been working together with Emilia’s group during the time she’d traded places with Echidna, when she’d been sleeping at the depths of her own consciousness.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">It went without saying for Echidna, whom she’d known for around ten years, but even the practically brand spanking new Julius, at least as far as her memories were concerned, could easily tell that she was shook.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">On that point, it was quite the surprise to Anastasia that Beatrice, who should have been quite close to them, had come to this meeting.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [Beatrice-chan, are ya alright? Ah wait, no, it ain’t propa’ of me to use a word like “Alright”. But still, ya haven’ gotten ya’self into a panic, no?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: […Betty panicking wouldn’t mean it’d bring back Subaru and Rem, in fact. Right now, the main issue in question is messing up our first move due to panic, I suppose. I’d specifically like to avoid that, in fact.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [First move? By that, do you mean…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: […Bringing back Subaru and Rem. Taking action for that, yeah?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia raised her eyebrows quizzically as she tried to question Beatrice. However Emilia interrupted her, still while supporting Ram by her shoulder.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia’s amethyst eyes pierced right through Anastasia, her eyes widening in surprise a tad at the intensity of her glare. She wet her lips with her tongue.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [Dang, I thought you were gonna be dejected as heck, but that’s one helluva’ look you’ve got in your eyes. What d’ya mean?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: [It’s really nothing complicated. Subaru and Rem ended up being swallowed by that shadow and taken away from us… Volcanica did drive it away, but he was just a tad too slow. Even so…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram: [They didn’t die from it. I’m sure of it.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">While still being propped up by Emilia, Ram opened her eyes and replied. Then she gently touched the part of her on her forehead ― the part where presumably her Oni Clan Horn had been ― as she let out an exhausted sigh. It was what served as the basis of her words, which felt full of conviction.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [The Oni Clan’s… Ah, wouldn’t that be the sisters’ Synesthesia? So you’re saying that you’re linked with Rem-san?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram: [Yep, that’s right. Rem is alive… No clue about Barusu.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: [Subaru’s alright as well, I suppose! Betty can guarantee that, in fact!]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice shouted in anger, almost red in the face, in response to Ram’s very Ram-like words. After all, she was Beatrice, the Spirit who’d made a Contract with Subaru. Her words did have some credibility to them.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">In other words, it meant that the two of them who had vanished had been confirmed to be alive from separate angles.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius: [In regards to Subaru, we have his connection with Beatrice-sama’s Contract, and in regards to Miss Rem, we have Miss Ram’s Synesthesia… Both of those are worth believing in. Besides, I too would like to hope.]&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [Quite the honesty there… Well, either way, I too would rather prefer that Natsuki-kun ‘n Rem-san were safe ‘n sound.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia stroked her scarf after Julius replied, as if to say getting </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">too </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">close to them would be a problem.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">They were working with Emilia’s group as collaborators for the time being ― But the fact that they were competing with each other in the Royal Selection hadn’t changed. She didn’t want them to get the wrong idea about that and add to their troubles.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Nevertheless, it was </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">precisely </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">because they were fighting each other that they ought to repay their debts without fail.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meili: [We~ll then? Like, it’s super-duper good that Onii-san ‘n stuff are alive, bu~t, where are they?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Failing to read the mood positively, Meili asked them that while playing around with the Tiny Crimson Scorpion balanced on her head. Julius opened his mouth in reaction to her question, and began to speak,</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius: [Well, though I wouldn’t be able to explain them in great detail, that shadow was the product of Yin Magic… Or at the very least, the Yin Attribute. It should be the same sort that’s sealing the likes of the Witch of Envy in her shrine, and Roy Alphard in the Dragon Carriage. If we presume this to be the case, then as for our resident expert…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: […That would be Betty, I suppose. My prognosis isn’t all that different from Julius’ either, in fact. That </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">thing</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> was a clump of Yin, I suppose. It’s close to a Shamak that’s been given direction, in fact.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Echidna: [Shamak? Isn’t that the basic magic of the Yin Attribute? How was it then so powerful?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: […I knew there were going to be quite a few </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">humans</span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;"> who’d misunderstand since there are hardly any users of it. However, it’s troubling that even </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">you </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">think of it like that, when you’re a Spirit like me, in fact.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Echidna, who had taken up her white-fox form, was stared at reproachfully by Beatrice, causing her to cower her head. Giving a sigh at her reaction, Beatrice raised one of her fingers and said: “May I, I suppose?”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: [Shamak’s special quality is in “Separation”, in fact. A low-grade Shamak separates one’s body from their awareness, whereas a high-grade Shamak rends space from itself, I suppose. Betty’s Door Crossing is one such application, linking two rooms together, and achieving something close to teleportation, in fact.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram: [That was the contraption Beatrice-sama had set up in the previous mansion. It was a real pain having to call her for mealtimes, so to be quite honest, I’d gotten fed up with it.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: [It really upsets Betty that you’d say that so honestly right here, right now, I suppose. I do believe I am sorry about it, but the apologies can wait until later… Anyways, Shamak, in essence, can separate space from space if so inclined; as well as that, it can separate objects, no matter how strong they are. It’s the Witch of Envy’s speciality, in fact.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [From your explanation, aren’t you saying that Natsuki-kun and Rem-san have been scattered into pieces?]</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">If </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">that shadow was as deadly as that, then what she’d said felt just like something that branded Subaru and Rem’s survival, who’d been swallowed by it, as being hopeless.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice however shook her head at Anastasia’s question.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: [What I mentioned right now was an allegory, I suppose. At least, that shadow wasn’t something that was set to chop Subaru and Rem into pieces, in fact. But it was going to take them within and whisk them away somewhere, I suppose.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Echidna: [Somewhere… Across space… In this situation, I imagine it would be to where she is?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram: […After all, there’s the matter of Barusu’s Miasma.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Echidna broke down Beatrice’s exposition into simpler terms, and at the end of that, Ram provided the reason why.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Miasma that enveloped Subaru, which had greatly agitated the Witchbeasts en-route to the Watchtower, was now a well-known fact amongst the Royal Selection Candidates.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Regarding this Miasma thing, both its cause, and its finer details, were unknowns.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram: [All I know about the Miasma is that it attracts Witchbeasts to it, and that the people who are connected with the Witch Cult often emanate it in spades… That’s pretty much it.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: [Hm, although Subaru’s been chased by Witchbeasts quite a few times, he’s got nothing to do with the Witch Cult. I get that it’s preeeeetty suspicious, but…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [No need ta fret, don’ worry, I ain’t castin’ doubt on that now, Emilia-san.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: [Oh? Thank goodness. Subaru is a good boy, so…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia patted her chest in relief, however, the issue was still very much there.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Just like Anastasia had said, the thread of thought regarding Subaru being connected to the Witch Cult was no longer inside her. Nevertheless, the fact that they didn’t know Subaru’s origins still brought forth doubts within her.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">She didn’t think that Subaru was someone with ill intent. However, ill intent that one wasn’t conscious to was a thing too.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">There was a chance that, behind all of his good intent, Subaru’s existence was a ticking time-bomb in itself.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [Yeah Yeah, </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">so your concern is unnecessary, </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">I got that much from how ya started the sentence off. The shadow that swallowed Natsuki-kun and Rem-san up was the one that whisked ‘em away ta somewhere. The real question is, where ta?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Echidna: [As well as what happened due to Volcanica’s breath having interfered with it.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice and Ram, who were linked with the two of Subaru and Rem respectively, probably had a rough idea of their whereabouts.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Searching for them was going to be a tall order, if all they knew was that they were alive, without knowing exactly where they’d been hurtled off to―― At the very least, the option of not searching for them was not available to anyone in this place.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: [Please, you two, where are Subaru and Rem? Tell me please.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice and Ram exchanged looks, having turned towards Emilia’s sincere appeal. Then after a few seconds of silence from the both of them…</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Beatrice: […He’s south, in fact.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Ram: [Ram too senses that she’s in that same direction. I’m not sure of her exact position, but I think she’s fairly far away.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: [South? South from here we have the uhh…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia furrowed her well-defined eyebrows straight after Beatrice and Ram had both mentioned they were south. Though she must have been giving it her all to envision the map of the world in her mind, Anastasia raised her hand in her stead.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Currently they were in the Augria Sand Dunes situated in the easternmost part of the world map. As far as south from here went, Picoutatte or Flanders, of the five great cities, were the most likely, however…</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius: [In regards to that alone, the state of the two is inscrutable.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Seemingly having arrived at the same idea, Julius spoke Anastasia’s mind for her.&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">From the way he got into sync with the speed of her thoughts, Anastasia valued him as being ideal as her knight even more, all while batting one of her eyes shut and saying: “Indeed.”</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [‘Tis quite the distance, but if they find themselves aroun’ those parts, then we can say that the damage incurred would be negligible. Yet, despite that, both of yer faces look pale… Do ya both mean ta say he’s way more south?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: [Way more south, you can’t mean…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meili: […They ended up going all the way down to the Vollachian Empire in the sou~th?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Meili’s surprise was minimal compared to how shocked Emilia was. But it was safe to say that Meili’s reaction was down to the fact that she hadn’t comprehended the scale of things. Given that, Beatrice and the others’ reactions were all perfectly understandable.</span></p>
<p><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">If </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">Subaru and Rem had crossed through the country… And </span><i><span style="font-weight: 400;">if </span></i><span style="font-weight: 400;">they’d crossed into the Southern Empire, they had a real problem at hand.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Anastasia: [If I’m not mistaken… The Kingdom of Lugunica ‘n the Vollachian Empire in the south signed a nonaggression pact that would last for the entire Royal Selection. Isn’ that right, Julius?]&nbsp;</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius: [That’s correct. I imagine you wouldn’t know about this, but I also went there to tie down the agreement. Me, Reinhard, Ferris and such met the Emperor in person.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: [From what I’d heard, we were on reaaaally bad terms with the Empire; but, if you did that, then are we okay with them now? So if we were to go and search for Subaru and Rem…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius: […No, that’s not going to fly.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius lowered his brows with a shake of his head in response to Emilia’s timid question. He softly cast his yellow eyes down, which glittered like jewels, emblazoned on his handsome features,</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius: [The Empire has forbidden travel between itself and the Kingdom since several months ago, for the time being. Due to the Royal Selection, the same thing happened with the Holy Kingdom of Gusteko in the north, however they’ve already rescinded their ban…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Echidna: [I guess that means the one the Empire set hasn’t been rescinded. Though we did travel to the Watchtower, so because of that we don’t have the latest information… So if the situation there hasn’t changed…]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Emilia: [Going there to help Subaru and Rem is going to be tough?]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Julius: [Yes, unfortunately.]</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Though they exchanged various thoughts about it, Julius concluded with these mere words of regret.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Welling up fever and pain he took notice of after a delay, spread through his entire body, and blood streamed out of Subaru’s eyes, nose, and ears as well.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Tasting the sensation of the disappearance of oneself and becoming empty, whilst feeling icy “Death” draw close to him, Subaru strained his eyes, made his throat tremble, and called her name until the end.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">――Called her name, until the end.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Smeared in blood.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Until the very end of the end, he called, called, kept calling her name.</span></p>
